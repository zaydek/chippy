# Changelog

## Coming next

- No more API keys: your invite link is all you need.
- The two modes are now called Talk (you speak) and Chat (you type). The old names still work for a while.
- Copy the important part of an answer to your clipboard by asking.
- Save the last few minutes of a conversation as a clip, if you turn that on.
- Pause and play, and buttons to go back or forward a sentence or a whole message.
- Extra voices: Claude can give lines to more than one voice, each with its own speed and volume.
- Voice cloning: add a person's voice, only with them there and agreeing out loud.

## 0.8.3

- Feedback works right after installing, with nothing to set up.
- The setup check now tells you when your computer may be too slow for Talk.

## 0.8.2

- If a voice fails for a moment, Chippy tries again before switching to its backup voice.
- The short sentence Claude says before it starts working is now spoken right away.

## 0.8.1

- The short sentence Claude says before it starts working is spoken as soon as it's written.
